This perspective yields an offline-online decomposition: the operator is learned once from simulated data and subsequently reused across all exercise dates, eliminating the need to recompute regression models at each step of the backward recursion. We establish convergence guarantees and derive bounds quantifying the approximation errors across exercise dates. Numerical experiments demonstrate the speed and accuracy of the proposed approach relative to extant methods.
