Abstract: We review the discrete theory of differential-geometric Poisson brackets as introduced by B. A. Dubrovin, and we present a complete proof of their characterisation in the non-degenerate case. We use this characterisation to show several novel examples of such structures, providing a complete classification for non-degenerate four-dimensional differential-geometric Poisson brackets under the additional assumption of the Lie algebra to be of quasi-Frobenius type. We also present a complete classification of quasi-Frobenius filiform N-graded Lie algebras, including two families in arbitrary dimensions.
